# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We want LocalSift to be a welcoming and inclusive space. We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
* Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
* Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
